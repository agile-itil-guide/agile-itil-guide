Welcome to Agile ITIL Guide Discussions

Agile ITIL Guide now has GitHub Discussions, a place to ask questions, suggest ideas, and share what you have done with it.

Agile ITIL Guide is a practical handbook for running IT services the agile way, with research, practices, examples, ideas to talk about with teams, AI skills, and AI prompts. It is written for delivery teams, service desk and operations staff, product and service owners, agile coaches, and IT managers.

## What this means for you

- You can ask questions and get answers from other readers.
- You can suggest topics, fixes, and improvements before anyone writes them.
- You can share how you use the guide.

## Where to go for what

- Questions about using the guide: Q&A
- Suggestions for new or changed topics: Ideas
- Quick community votes: Polls
- Things you have built or tried with the guide: Show and tell
- Everything else: General
- Errors, broken links, or typos: Issues (https://github.com/agile-itil-guide/agile-itil-guide/issues)
