Summary:
The Agile ITIL Guide is a free, open handbook that shows IT teams how to run live services the agile way. It combines the Agile Manifesto, Scrum, Kanban, Lean and DevOps with ITIL 4 practices such as incident, problem, change and service desk. Anyone can read it online today. The problem it solves is simple: teams that deliver fast and teams that keep services stable rarely share a plan.

Benefits:
Delivery teams get a shared language with operations. Service desk staff see how fast feedback and stable service fit together. Managers find a maturity table for every topic, so a team can say where it stands and choose a next step. Each topic includes a worked example, discussion questions and prompts that you can copy and paste. One example follows a small team as it organizes on-call duty, and another shows how to prioritize problems after a rough week.

Why this matters:
Agile and ITIL often sound like rivals. One promises speed and the other promises control. Real services need both. A team that ships weekly still needs a calm way to handle an outage at midnight. This guide treats the two as partners and gives teams concrete practices to try on Monday morning.
